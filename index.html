Bespoke Creation Process

How Your Masterpiece Is Born

From raw forest timber to your living sanctuary — every piece at Rajesh Furniture Works is an unhurried labor of love and generational precision.

01

Consultation & 3D Blueprinting

We discuss your spatial needs, architectural aesthetics, lifestyle preferences, and take laser-accurate site measurements to generate bespoke 3D renders.

Concept & Dimensions
02

Kiln-Seasoned Hardwood Curation

Each plank of Grade-A CP Teakwood, Sheesham, or Oak is hand-inspected for natural grain continuity, vacuum pressure-treated, and kiln-dried to optimal 8-12% moisture.

Moisture & Termite Shield
03

Artisan Joinery & Hand-Carving

Our senior master carpenters sculpt mortise-and-tenon interlocking joints, dovetails, fluted acoustic panels, and intricate relief carvings with zero synthetic fillers.

Generational Skill
04

Multi-Coat Polish & Home Fitting

Finished with premium Italian Polyurethane silk matte or organic Danish wax oils, transported in protective multi-layer packaging, and fitted seamlessly by our install crew.

Flawless Delivery
Material Authenticity

Our Wood Selection Guide

We never use particle board, hollow honeycomb cardboard, or low-grade MDF. Every creation starts with genuine kiln-dried logs.

100% Non-Toxic & Termite Proof

Grade-A Indian CP Teak

Sourced: Madhya Pradesh / Nilambur
50+ Years (Natural Oils)

Renowned as the king of hardwoods. High natural silica and teak oil content make it naturally immune to termites, moisture warping, and wood rot. Rich golden-brown patina that grows deeper with age.

Prime Recommendation:Master Beds, Temple Mandirs, Main Doors

Indian Sheesham (Rosewood)

Sourced: North & Central India
35+ Years High Hardness

Celebrated for its dramatic dark-and-light grain contrast and exceptional load-bearing density. Perfect for heavy structural furniture and dining surfaces where natural grain depth is desired.

Prime Recommendation:Dining Tables, Coffee Tables, Heavy Consoles

BWP High-Density Marine Core

Sourced: IS:710 Certified Marine Grade
25+ Years 100% Boiling Waterproof

Bonded with unextended phenol formaldehyde resin under intense hydraulic pressure. Guaranteed zero delamination, zero fungal rot, and zero swelling even in humid coastal or kitchen environments.

Prime Recommendation:Modular Kitchens, Wardrobe Internals, Vanities

European White Oak & Beech

Sourced: Sustainably Harvested FSC Timber
30+ Years Medium-Dense Grain

Straight open-grain structure that absorbs modern matte stains beautifully. Imparts a breezy, contemporary European atmosphere favored by modern interior architects.

Prime Recommendation:Scandinavian Slatted Panels, Modern Chairs
